# LeadGen Design System

## Direction

LeadGen should feel like an evidence desk, not a generic sales dashboard. The interface is dark, focused, and analytical, with small high-confidence color signals instead of decorative gradients or oversized card grids.

## Foundations

- **Typeface:** Manrope Variable for interface and display text
- **Ink:** `#0a0b0d` for the application background
- **Paper:** `#f4f2ec` for primary text and decisive actions
- **Muted text:** `#aaa69b`
- **Signal:** `#e763a9`, used for active discovery and focus
- **Verified:** `#c9f06b`, used only for qualified or confirmed states
- **Danger:** `#ff897d`
- **Radius:** 11–14px for fields and controls; pills only for compact statuses

## Layout

Authentication uses a split screen on desktop: product evidence and atmosphere on the left, focused action on the right. Below 820px, the evidence panel becomes a short masthead and the form occupies the full width.

## Interaction

- Every interactive control has a visible keyboard focus state.
- Motion is subtle and disabled when reduced motion is requested.
- Primary actions are light against the dark surface; Google remains a secondary outlined action.
- Errors explain the next corrective action and never rely on color alone.

## Voice

Use direct, concrete language. Prefer “evidence,” “signal,” and “reason” over claims about guaranteed intent. Demo facts must be clearly illustrative until backed by real data.
